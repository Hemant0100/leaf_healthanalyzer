# Sample photos

The `.jpg` files here are **synthetic OpenCV drawings** (green ellipse, yellow ellipse, green ellipse with dark dots). They exist only so the code runs before you add real pictures.

**Replace them with your own phone photos.** Keep the folder names:

- `healthy/` — green leaves, little or no yellow, no disease spots
- `yellowing/` — leaves that look pale or yellow
- `spotted/` — leaves with dark dots or brown patches
- `test/` — extra photos you do not use while tuning thresholds

Tips:

- One leaf per photo
- Plain background
- Daylight, top-down
- JPG or PNG is fine
